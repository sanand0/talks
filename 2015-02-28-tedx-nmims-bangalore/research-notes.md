# Research notes — 2015 TEDx NMIMS Bangalore election data story

**Editorial status (15 Oct 2026):** Desk research for a historical story based on a 2015 lecture and 2013–2014 election graphics. The original slides/actual video frames have not yet been audited here. Sources below are **external corroboration**, not evidence that Anand showed or quoted those pages on stage. Prefer original ECI, ADR and contemporary 2013–2014 coverage over later recollection for quantitative claims.

## Reliable source shelf

### Election Commission — maximum candidate count and deposits

**ECI official FAQ:** https://www.eci.gov.in/faq/1/7

- ECI answers the maximum-candidates question: **Modakurichi Assembly, Tamil Nadu, 1996, 1,033 candidates; ballot papers were a booklet.** This is the most useful external primary citation and directly supports the climax.
- The same FAQ discusses pre/post changes in candidate deposits: for an Assembly contest in or before 1996, a general-category candidate's deposit is listed as **₹250** and an SC/ST candidate's **₹125**; the contemporary listed deposit for general candidates is **₹10,000**. **Do not automatically interpret "₹500 to everybody" in Anand's anecdote as the election deposit**. And do not assert the 1996 protest *directly caused* the legal deposit change without establishing the precise legislative sequence.
- Official FAQ content is not a replacement for the 1996 constituency-level results. For any attempt to reconstruct the complete names/votes or a historical map, locate the original constituency-statistical report and disclose extraction/cleaning methodology.

### Contemporary affidavit evidence — criminal cases and the protest connection

**ADR-hosted 2014 news coverage of the NEW/ADR report:** https://adrindia.org/index.php/media/adr-in-news/ls-polls-6th-phase-two-aap-candidates-tamil-nadu-face-762-cases

**National Election Watch primary press statement archived on group:** https://groups.google.com/g/national-election-watch/c/dMdMIHZg7BA

**MyNeta 2014 candidate sorting:** https://myneta.info/ls2014/index.php?action=summary&page=1&sort=criminal&subAction=candidates_analyzed

- The ADR/NEW press statement confirms two AAP 2014 Lok Sabha candidates: **S. P. Udayakumar, Kanniyakumari: 382 declared criminal cases; M. Pushparayan, Thoothukkudi: 380**.
- Contemporary reporting identifies both as anti-nuclear activists involved with protests against the Kudankulam nuclear power project.
- **Declared criminal cases ≠ convictions**. Also, "activist" does not automatically establish that every filed case arose from a protest or was trivial.
- These are two candidates' counts. Avoid presenting "AAP was the most criminal party" without properly defined denominators, jurisdiction, timeframe and severity. The original graphic may rank **case count**, not risk or conviction rates.
- Names in the transcript ("Dr Uday Kumar", "Pushparayan") should match the affidavits in the article; link rather than silently normalize a contested speaker quote.

### Doordarshi Party — large presence, no wins

**Contemporaneous Economic Times / Gramener infographic, 20 Mar 2014:** https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/politics-and-nation/lok-sabha-polls-parties-who-have-contested-repeatedly-and-won-few-or-no-seats/articleshow/32368439.cms

- This 2014 article credits "**Analysis & graphics by Gramener.com**" and reports **97** candidates in 1984, **298** in 1989, **321** in 1991, zero seats each year. Total **716** candidacies. It is unusually good *contemporary* evidence from the same analysis team behind the TEDx story.
- **Wikipedia** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doordarshi_Party gives **288** for 1989, conflicting with that 2014 figure. Prefer the original Gramener graphic/talk in an explicitly historical account, or recompute ECI original if publishing a new supposedly definitive statistic.
- Published summaries corroborate teetotal/vegetarian candidate requirements. The transcript's additional "bachelors" and possible "celibate" requirements are **speaker speculation** ("I think ... I'm not sure"), not established rules. Avoid repeating as researched fact.
- "Second largest party" in the talk means **by candidacies fielded in the 1989 election**, not vote share or seats. The party won zero Lok Sabha seats across those cycles; don't generalize to every local election.

### Modakurichi — protest, zero votes, and retrospective reporting

**2016 DT Next historical report:** https://www.dtnext.in/city/2016/04/21/when-1033-contestants-fought-for-modakurichi-seat-in-1996

**2021 New Indian Express interview with farmer organizer:** https://www.newindianexpress.com/amp/story/states/tamil-nadu/2021/Mar/05/tamil-nadu-polls-flashback-when-modakurichi-farmers-made-1996-election-historic-2272379.html

**2026 New Indian Express retrospective:** https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/tamil-nadu/2026/Mar/26/1033-candidates-65-page-booklet-and-one-bizarre-day-of-polling-in-erode

- Secondary reporting independently repeats **1,033 candidates** (the ECI primary FAQ agrees). 2016 report also lists **88** with zero votes and **158** with one; 2021 report recalls **88** who did not vote for themselves but sometimes gives **161** in one-vote category, demonstrating recall/extraction differences.
- 2021 reporting explicitly identifies a **farmers' protest**, and quotes organizer C. Nallasamy. Retrospective quote: *"Our goal was not to win but to be heard."* That is a **2021 interview quotation**, **NOT** something said on stage in 2015. Use it only with its 2021 date/attribution if desired.
- Contemporary and later reports differ on length of printed booklet: **50 pages** (2021) versus **65 pages** (2026). Prefer "booklet" (ECI's wording). Avoid reproducing an alleged exact ballot booklet without a traceable source.
- Reports discuss election scheduling and deposit requirements changed afterward; causality and precise timeline deserve independent verification before turning it into a neat reform narrative.
- The speaker's "88 people couldn't find themselves" is humor, **not** the documented reason for zero votes. The underlying strategy is the meaningful twist.
- Do not reproduce large copyrighted photographs from journalism or a fabricated official ballot. Authentic original slides/recording are best visual evidence.

### 2014 Chandu Sahu namesakes — observation versus accusation

**Hindustan Times, 28 Mar 2014:** https://www.hindustantimes.com/india/bjp-nominee-faces-10-namesakes-has-ajit-jogi-played-a-trick/story-8JfyzOr9xXJxuOeBywK7yN.html

**Indian Express, 16 Apr 2014:** https://indianexpress.com/article/political-pulse/jogi-plays-his-cards-many-dummies-and-a-daughter-in-law/

- The first reports **11 similarly named Chandu Sahus** including variants **Chandu/Chanduram**, not 11 people all with the complete legal name "Chandulal Sahu". The BJP alleged an attempt to confuse voters; Ajit Jogi denied knowledge when asked in the same contemporaneous account.
- An Indian Express contemporaneous account describes multiple similar names and alleges political staging, but **journalistic attribution is not verified responsibility**.
- Anand's last line is carefully qualified: he is "just making an observation." The article should model equally careful attribution. Do not imply Congress as an organization orchestrated a fraudulent scheme on the basis of this chart alone.
- The archived results describe a tight 2014 contest, but election outcome does not retrospectively prove the alleged plan.

### Faridabad bellwether and other trivia

- Anand reports Faridabad as having elected the nationally victorious party every time through **2014**. Independently checking the strong **"only constituency"** claim requires constituency creation dates, boundary/delimitation definitions and a repeatable historical result table. **Do not publish it as a verified unique timeless law** based only on the talk.
- Nine-vote contests (1998, 1999), only-women elections, the oldest candidate and 90/250 intermediate Tamil Nadu data points are **only transcript-supported here**. Present them as questions Anand demonstrated, or verify from ECI/statistical returns before asserting broadly.
- Constituency name written "Muthandikuppam [?]" in transcript is uncertain. Check the original graphic/video before choosing a spelling.

## Source interpretation rules for the eventual story

Use **three visible levels** of claims:

1. **"In the 2015 talk, Anand showed/said…"** — exact historical speech and visual.
2. **"The affidavit/ECI record/reported interview says…"** — externally grounded facts, with the linked report and the relevant election year.
3. **"This suggests…"** — modern editorial interpretation; don't put it in Anand's mouth or present it as a measured causal effect.

If a number conflicts across sources, the reader should see the **relevant conflict at the point where it matters**, not merely a blanket disclaimer at the bottom. Resist presentist claims such as "2026 AI has solved this data problem" or current political party performance. This article is about what exploratory election visualizations made possible **in 2013–2015**.
