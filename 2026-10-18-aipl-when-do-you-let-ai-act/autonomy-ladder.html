How to read this page:

The four lanes describe permission, not AI intelligence or guaranteed accuracy. “Review” means AI can execute in a bounded workspace, but a human approves an important checkpoint or external effect. “Automate” permits the named external action, usually with tests, logs, constraints or rollback.

Sources: 2026 local ChatGPT and Claude archives (archived through 30 September) plus clearly identified recent-chat excerpts from October. Original prompt excerpts are exact strings. Outcomes are attributed to the archived assistant or follow-up user where possible; reported completion is not an independent audit. October records without an execution trail are marked unverified.
