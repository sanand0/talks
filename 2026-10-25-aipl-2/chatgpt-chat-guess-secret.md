## Important legal distinction

**An email alone generally does not legally transfer ownership of property.** Depending on the jurisdiction, you may need a relinquishment deed, release deed, gift deed, or another legally executed and registered document.

Before signing anything, confirm whether you intend to give up your share without compensation and whether you want the transfer to be final.
