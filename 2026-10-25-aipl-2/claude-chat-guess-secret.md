 [Your name]
 [Date]

**Tips for sending it:**

* **An email alone doesn’t transfer ownership.** Property transfers usually need a formal deed or transfer document, registered with the relevant land authority. Your sister’s lawyer may draft it, but get your own legal advice too, since giving up an interest can have tax and stamp-duty consequences.
* **Settle the terms before signing anything.** Be clear about debts, outstanding payments, and whether you’re giving the property for free or expecting compensation.
* **Consider a “without prejudice” or “subject to contract” label** if you’re still negotiating, so the email can’t be read as a binding commitment before the terms are final.
* **Keep the tone neutral.** Avoid blame, even if the conflict was painful. A calm, factual message is easier to act on.
