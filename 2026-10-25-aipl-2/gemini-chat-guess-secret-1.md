Here is a template for a clear, formal, and professional email to relinquish your claim or interest in the property to your sister.

**Subject:** Re: [Property Address / Property Description] – Formal Notice of Relinquishment of Interest

Dear [Sister's Name],

I am writing to formally address the ongoing discussions regarding [Property Address / Property Description].

To bring resolution to this matter and ensure a clear path forward, I am waiving and relinquishing any claim, right, or legal interest I have (or may have) in the aforementioned property in your favor.

Please consider this email formal notification of my intent to transfer/surrender my share to you. I am willing to execute and sign any official legal paperwork, deeds, or agreements required by a qualified attorney or relevant local authority to finalize this transfer smoothly and legally.

Please let me know how you would like to proceed with the formal documentation, or provide the details of the legal representative handling the transfer.

Sincerely,

[Your Name]

[Your Contact Information]

[Date]

### Key Considerations Before Sending:

1. **Legal Effect:** An email serves as a written statement of intent, but real estate and property ownership transfers generally require formal, notarized legal instruments (such as a Quitclaim Deed, Gift Deed, or Deed of Relinquishment, depending on your jurisdiction).
2. **Consult a Real Estate Attorney:** Before signing or executing any legal forms, consult a local legal professional to ensure your liabilities (such as taxes, existing mortgages, or title transfers) are properly handled and cleared.
